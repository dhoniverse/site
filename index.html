Discover Dhoniverse

Dhoniverse is a three-day cultural and adventure festival held in Dhoni, a picturesque village in Palakkad, Kerala.

Dhoni is surrounded by lush green hills, flowing streams, and the soothing sounds of nature — rustling bamboo leaves, gentle waterfalls, and radiant sunsets that color the sky. Known for its serene beauty and deep-rooted traditions, Dhoni represents one of Kerala's hidden gems.

Dhoniverse is more than a festival — it's a celebration of community, art, and nature, created by the people of Dhoni themselves. It's not just an event; it's an emotion.

Event Highlights

Experience a blend of tradition and thrill

Theyyam Performance

Kalaripayattu

Experience Kerala's ancient martial art — a mesmerizing display of strength, agility, and discipline.

Cycling Marathon

Cycle through scenic village paths and challenging terrains in an adrenaline-pumping competition.

Kolam Varakkal

Kabbaddi

Join the traditional Indian sport that tests strategy, strength, and teamwork in intense matches.

Gatta Gusthi

Watch traditional wrestling competitions that showcase raw power and ancient techniques.

Mentalism

Be amazed by mind-reading performances and psychological illusions that defy explanation.

Kaala Performance

Experience dynamic cultural performances that blend music, dance, and storytelling.
